Play-to-Earn Gaming

Grab rewards in 2P battles! Next-gen "Play & Earn" real-time P2P battle platform with skill and strategy. In InfaXia, players compete against each other in games to win victory rewards and NFTs. Tournament formats and prize battles create a "battle economy" that goes beyond just gaming.

Games & Rewards
  • RPS・Poker・Baccarat games

A multi-use market for the Web3 era. A next-gen store where all values from NFT items to real services can be traded. A new economic space where individuals, creators, and brands can freely open stores and achieve monetization and community building simultaneously.
